yt-analytics

yt-analytics is a private, read-only tool that Pradipta Mahardhika uses to see how the Shorts on their own YouTube channel, Tomorrow Tour, are performing.

It runs on the owner's own computer. It signs in with Google and asks only for read-only access to YouTube channel details and YouTube Analytics. With that, it builds a daily report of views, watch time and audience retention, so the owner can see which Shorts hold viewers and which lose them.

yt-analytics never uploads, edits or deletes anything on YouTube. It is not offered to the public, and it only reads channels that the signed-in Google account owns.
