yt-analytics privacy policy

Effective 6 November 2026

yt-analytics is a private tool that Pradipta Mahardhika runs on their own computer to read analytics for the YouTube channels they own. This page explains what it accesses and what happens to that data.

What it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, yt-analytics asks for two read-only permissions:

  • youtube.readonly: the channel's name, handle and list of uploaded videos.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: YouTube Analytics reports for those videos, such as views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources and viewer countries.

It cannot upload, edit, comment on or delete anything. It does not read any other Google data.

How the data is used

The data is used only to build performance reports for the channel owner. Those reports show which videos hold viewers and which lose them. Nothing is used for advertising, and nothing is sold.

Where it is stored

The Google sign-in token and the reports are stored on the owner's own computer. The owner may read the reports with tools of their choosing, such as an AI assistant that works on their behalf. No data is shared with anyone else, and none of it is transferred to third parties.

Google API Services

yt-analytics's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. yt-analytics uses YouTube API Services. By signing in you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and data

You can remove yt-analytics's access at any time from your Google account's third-party access page. Removing access stops all future reads. To delete the stored data, the owner deletes the sign-in token and report files from their computer.

Contact
